## L1 · 1. 结果补语“好”

**结构 Pattern:** V + 好

Shows an action is finished and done well or ready.

**Examples**

- 饭做好了，快来吃吧。 — The food is ready, come eat.
- 我的作业还没写好。 — My homework isn't finished yet.
- 你准备好了吗？ — Are you ready?

**练一练 Practise**

1. 用“结果补语“好””说一个关于你周末的句子。
2. 把下面的句子改成用这个结构的句子，然后读给朋友听。

**Common mistakes**

- Learners often drop part of the pattern in fast speech. Check that every required element of “V + 好” is present.
- Keep time words before the verb.

## L1 · 2. 一……也／都 + 没／不

**结构 Pattern:** 一 + M + N + 也/都 + 不/没 + V

Emphatic negation: not even one.

**Examples**

- 我一分钱也没有。 — I don't have a single cent.
- 他一句话都没说。 — He didn't say a word.
- 冰箱里一个鸡蛋也没有了。 — There isn't one egg left in the fridge.

## L2 · 3. 时间词的位置

**结构 Pattern:** Subj + 时间 + V / 时间 + Subj + V

Time words go before the verb, either before or after the subject, never at the end of the sentence.

**Examples**

- 我昨天去了超市。 — I went to the supermarket yesterday.
- 明天我们一起吃饭吧。 — Let's eat together tomorrow.
- 他每天晚上跑步。 — He runs every evening.

## L4 · 5. 因为……所以……

## L5 · 6. 比 comparison

**结构 Pattern:** A + 比 + B + Adj (+ 一点儿/得多)

Compares two things of the same kind. Do not put 很 or 非常 before the adjective; use 一点儿, 得多, 多了 after it.

**Examples**

- 这个手机比那个贵一点儿。 — This phone is a bit more expensive than that one.
- 今天比昨天冷得多。 — Today is much colder than yesterday.
- 我哥哥比我高。 — My older brother is taller than me.

## L6 · 8. 如果……就……

**结构 Pattern:** 如果 + 条件，(Subj) + 就 + 结果

Conditional: if ... then ... . 就 comes after the subject of the second clause.

**Examples**

- 如果明天不下雨，我们就去爬山。 — If it doesn't rain tomorrow, we'll go hiking.
- 如果你有问题，就给我打电话。 — If you have questions, call me.

## L9 · 11. 把字句

**结构 Pattern:** Subj + 把 + O + V + 其他成分

Disposal construction: what the subject does to a specific object. The verb needs something after it (complement, 了, location).

**Examples**

- 请把门关上。 — Please close the door.
- 我把作业放在桌子上了。 — I put the homework on the table.

## L15 · 14. 除了 A（以外），还 B

**结构 Pattern:** 除了 A（以外），Subj + 还 + B

Besides A, also B: B adds to A. Compare 除了 A（以外），都 B = everyone/everything except A.

**Examples**

- 除了中文以外，我还会说一点儿日语。 — Besides Chinese, I can also speak a little Japanese.
- 除了画画，他还喜欢做饭。 — Besides drawing, he also likes cooking.
- 除了小王，大家都来了。 — Everyone came except Xiao Wang.
